Anchor Voice - Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

The short version: Anchor Voice does not store, save, or retain any of your audio recordings, transcriptions, or personal data. Everything is processed in real time and immediately discarded.

1. Information We Process

Anchor Voice processes the following data temporarily and in real time only:

  • Audio recordings you choose to analyse within the app
  • Voice characteristics (volume, pacing, pitch) extracted during analysis
  • Transcription text generated from your audio for language pattern detection

All of this data is processed in memory during your session and is never saved, stored, or logged on our servers.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store audio recordings
  • We do not store transcriptions or message content
  • We do not collect personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • We do not require user accounts or login
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising tools
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties
  • We do not maintain message history or conversation logs

3. Third-Party Services

Anchor Voice uses the following third-party APIs for real-time processing:

  • OpenAI Whisper API - For speech-to-text transcription. Audio is sent to OpenAI's servers for transcription only. OpenAI's data usage policy applies. The transcription result is used for analysis and is not stored by Anchor.
  • Anthropic Claude API - For generating de-escalation suggestions. Only anonymised analysis results (e.g., "raised voice detected") are sent to Anthropic, never your actual words or recordings.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of OpenAI and Anthropic.

4. Data Retention

Anchor Voice follows a strict no-storage, no-history philosophy:

  • Audio data is deleted from memory immediately after analysis
  • Transcriptions are discarded after pattern detection completes
  • Analysis results exist only in your current app session
  • Closing the app clears all session data

5. Permissions

Anchor Voice requests the following device permissions:

  • Microphone - Required to record voice messages for analysis. Recordings are processed locally and via API, then discarded.

6. Children's Privacy

Anchor Voice is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly process data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
